1. Bandwidth Sharing: Monetize Your Idle Internet Connection

Bandwidth sharing platforms utilize your spare internet connectivity to help corporate clients perform SEO research, web scraping, and content delivery verification. The app runs quietly on your PC, Mac, or Android device without accessing personal data. Payouts are directly proportional to the amount of data processed through your node and your geographic location.
