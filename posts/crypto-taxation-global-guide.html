1. Capital Gains Tax vs. Income Tax

Tax agencies typically classify crypto transactions into two categories:

  • Capital Gains Tax: Applicable when you sell, trade, or spend cryptocurrency that has appreciated in value relative to your initial cost basis.
  • Income Tax: Applicable when you receive cryptocurrency as rewards (such as mining payouts, staking yields, DeFi interest, or employer salaries). The income value is calculated based on market price at receipt.

2. Tax Optimization: Tax-Loss Harvesting

A powerful legal tool to reduce tax liabilities is **Tax-Loss Harvesting**. If you hold underperforming assets (unrealized losses), you can sell them before the tax year ends to realize the losses. These capital losses can offset your capital gains from stocks or other crypto assets, lowering your overall tax bracket.
