Ethereum Danksharding: The Technical Roadmap to Sub-Cent Transaction Fees

Author: Starrope • Jun 24, 2026 • Read Time: ~6 min

Ethereum's mainnet is highly secure but can become congested and expensive during periods of high demand. To scale, Ethereum's roadmap focuses on Layer 2 rollups. The key upgrade to support this modular scaling is **Danksharding**. By introducing a new way to store temporary rollup data, Danksharding aims to lower transaction fees and increase capacity. Here is the technical breakdown.

1. EIP-4844: Proto-Danksharding and Blobs

The first major step toward this scaling is EIP-4844, also known as **Proto-Danksharding**. This upgrade introduced a new transaction type that carries 'blobs' of data. Blobs are large packets of data that are processed by the Ethereum consensus layer but are not stored permanently on-chain, automatically deleting after approximately 18 days.

Because blobs do not compete with regular mainnet execution transactions for permanent storage, they are much cheaper, allowing Layer 2 networks to settle transactions on Ethereum at a fraction of the previous cost.

"Proto-Danksharding has made Layer 2 transactions cheaper than ever. It sets the foundation for Ethereum to support global-scale consumer applications."

2. Transitioning to Full Danksharding

Full Danksharding will expand this capacity by implementing **Data Availability Sampling (DAS)**. This allows nodes to verify that blob data is complete without having to download the entire file, enabling Ethereum to handle hundreds of blobs per block and support sub-cent transaction fees across the ecosystem.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is Proto-Danksharding (EIP-4844)?

An Ethereum upgrade that introduced 'data-carrying transactions' or blobs, which are temporary data pockets dedicated to Layer 2 rollups.

How do blobs reduce fees?

Rollup data was previously stored permanently on-chain as Calldata; blobs store data temporarily and delete it after a few weeks, lowering gas fees up to 90%.

When will full Danksharding be implemented?

Full Danksharding is a multi-year roadmap that will split blob data storage across multiple nodes, further increasing network capacity.
